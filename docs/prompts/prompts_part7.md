3) pressed (darker, pushed in, shadow on top), 4) disabled (grey faded wood).

6) a small empty tab label made of cardboard with brown tape on top (inactive),
7) the same tab label, active (brighter, a red postal stamp mark in the corner),
8) a title banner ribbon in red with golden edges, empty middle.
Row 3 -
9) a slider track: a thin wooden groove, horizontal,
